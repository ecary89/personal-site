Foundations

Tokens

One file, tokens.css, holds every value. The same tokens ship as tokens.json for Figma.

Color

Paper, plum ink and one rose accent. Rose marks what to look at or click. The tag under each text color is its contrast on paper. Body text needs 4.5:1.

Supporting colors for charts and controls: --rose-tint, --neutral-tint, --neutral, --control-off-bg, --control-off-fg, --control-pending.

Type

Two families. Bricolage Grotesque for names, titles, headings and quotes. Schibsted Grotesk for everything you read. Sizes are fluid between phone and desktop. All caps only for short labels that name something.

Space

A 4px grid, with 2px half steps next to type. Big gaps between blocks grow with the screen.

Fluid gaps: --space-tiles 64 → 112px, --space-section 64 → 104px, --space-footer 72 → 120px, --gap-columns 28 → 72px. Layout: content 1200px, intro 860px, reading 720px, side padding 24 → 64px.

Shape

Rounded, never sharp. Anything you click is a pill.

Depth

Shadows are tinted with ink and only lift screens off the wash or a button off the page.

Motion

Blocks fade in and rise. Washes fade in behind links. Buttons lift 1px. Under Reduce Motion, fades stay and movement stops.

Media hero

A 16:10 rounded panel (4:3 on a phone). Screens float on the rose wash. Pick the variant by what the media is.

--film fills the panel with a video that carries its own wash. Every video autoplays muted, loops, plays inline and has an aria-label that says what happens in it.

Carousel

Slides crossfade. While the carousel is on screen, the arrow that will turn the page fills with rose. A click or a scroll inside stops it for good. It never cycles under Reduce Motion.

Password gate

One field. The password decides which page opens. The error line holds its space so nothing jumps. While a page decrypts, a rose curtain rises, covers the page, then lifts off as the page rises in behind it.
